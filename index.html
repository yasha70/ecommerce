Taxable amount from GST portal

Net taxable = gross value − value of supplies returned. The inter / intra-state split follows the IGST vs CGST + SGST share of each credit. Expected rate: TCS 1% up to June 2024, 0.5% from 10 July 2024 (July 2024 accepts either); TDS 2%.

Match with marketplace reports

Only TCS credits are matched. A mismatch usually means orders or returns the operator reported in a different month, or a GSTR-8 amendment — check that month's order-level report.
